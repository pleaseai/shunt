---
title: Advisor Gate
description: Keep one executor model on every turn and have a stronger advisor approve its plans and completion claims, or send it back to redo the work.
---

`type = "advisor"` keeps one **executor** serving every client-visible turn. A
stronger **advisor** reviews the executor's terminal turns — a plan before the
work, or a claim that the task is done — before the client sees them. APPROVE
releases the held turn. REDO discards it and sends the executor back to work with
the advisor's plan. The advisor never serves a turn, so the client only ever sees
executor output.

## Configure it

```toml
[[models]]
id = "claude-reviewed"

[models.router]
type = "advisor"
executor_target = "claude-sonnet-4-6"
advisor_target = "claude-opus-4-8"
gate_trigger = "no_tool_call"
max_reviews = 1
# gate_stall_turns = 0
# gate_min_tool_results = 0
# advisor_max_tokens = 2048
# transcript_max_chars = 200000
# fail_open = true
```

What fires a review:

- `gate_trigger = "no_tool_call"` (the default) reviews the executor's first turn
  that ends without a tool call. `gate_min_tool_results` makes such a turn
  reviewable only once the conversation carries that many tool results.
- `gate_trigger = "pattern"` fires on `gate_trigger_pattern` instead, a regex
  that is searched rather than anchored.
- `gate_stall_turns` additionally reviews one turn as a mid-task checkpoint once
  the conversation carries that many assistant turns; `0` turns it off.

`max_reviews` caps reviews per session. A request without
`x-claude-code-session-id` counts as a session of its own, so sessionless callers
never share one budget. The advisor sees the transcript trimmed from the middle to
`transcript_max_chars`, and `reviewer_system_prompt` and `redo_feedback_prefix`
replace the packaged APPROVE/REDO reviewer prompt and the text placed in front of
a REDO plan fed back to the executor.

The six [per-call bounds](/reference/configuration/#per-call-bounds) go on
`[models.router]`; the three `gated_*` bounds cap the held executor turn.

## Which turns are held

Whether a turn trips `gate_trigger` is known only once the turn is complete, so
while the session still has review budget **every** executor turn is held, and the
ones that do not trip the gate are served without a review. Once `max_reviews` is
spent, or the advisor has failed three consults in the session (a failed consult
refunds its review), the executor streams live with no buffering for the rest of
the session. [Held turns](/guides/routing/#held-turns) explains how a held turn is
served and what it costs: on a held turn the first byte arrives when the whole
turn is done.

## REDO

The held turn is discarded before any response header reaches the client. The
discarded turn and the advisor's plan are appended to the conversation, and the
executor is re-run. That re-run streams live.

## When a review fails

With `fail_open = true` (the default), a failed review serves the held turn.
`fail_open = false` fails the request with a `502` instead — also when the review
was never sent because the session spent `max_judge_calls`. Either way the failed
review is still counted under its outcome (e.g. `upstream_error`, `timeout`), or
as `budget_exhausted` when `max_judge_calls` refused it.

A held turn that crosses a `gated_*` bound or ends before its terminal marker is
discarded before any header is sent, and the request fails with a gateway-owned
`502` in the Anthropic error shape. It is not a REDO and not a failover attempt:
the upstream already answered `2xx`. The
[configuration reference](/reference/configuration/#buffered-turns-escalation-and-advisor)
tabulates every failure case.

## Credentials and probes

`executor_target` may be a **passthrough** route, although `advisor_target` may
not: the executor turn is the client's own answer, so it carries the caller's
credential, while a review is a judge call and carries none of it. See
[judge calls](/guides/routing/#judge-calls).

A `count_tokens` probe makes no review and no gated call, and always answers from
`executor_target`.

## What the client sees

| `x-gateway-route-source` | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| `advisor_approve` | The executor turn was reviewed and approved; it was replayed |
| `advisor_pass` | The executor turn was served without a review — it did not trip the gate, or no review could be reserved |
| `advisor_fail_open` | The review failed after a complete executor turn, so the turn was served |
| `advisor_redo` | The reviewer said REDO; this is the executor's live re-run |
| `advisor_exhausted` | Review budget is spent or the advisor failed three consults, so the executor streams with no buffering |
| `gated_error` | The gated turn could not be served |

Reviews are counted by `shunt.router.judge_calls{algorithm="advisor"}`, and
`GET /routes` lists `advisor_target` under `judges`. Every key is in the
[configuration reference](/reference/configuration/#type--advisor).
